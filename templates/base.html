Quick FAQs

How do I check my application status?
Click the Tracker link in the navigation bar, or visit the Application Tracker section in your Dashboard.
What documents are required?
Generally: Aadhaar card, income certificate, bank passbook, and any scheme-specific certificates. The AI Finder will list exact requirements after matching.
How long does approval take?
Processing times vary: Central schemes (15–45 days), Tamil Nadu schemes (7–21 days). Disbursement follows within 7 days of approval.
Can I apply for multiple schemes?
Yes! You can simultaneously apply for multiple eligible schemes. Our AI Finder helps you identify all schemes you qualify for.
